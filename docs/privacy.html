Contact SyncMate

Privacy Policy

Contact SyncMate · Effective 11 November 2025 · Last updated 10 August 2026

Summary: Contact SyncMate has no servers. Your contacts are never sent to, stored by, or processed by the developer. Contact data leaves your Mac only to reach Google — the service you connect — unless you explicitly enable optional AI-assisted matching, in which case limited fields from ambiguous duplicate pairs are sent to Anthropic (see section 5). Everything else happens locally on your Mac.

1. Information we do not collect

Contact SyncMate does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following to us, because we operate no servers:

  • Your contact information (names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, photos)
  • Your Google account credentials or OAuth tokens
  • Your Apple or iCloud account information
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports
  • Device information or identifiers
  • IP addresses or location data

Contact data leaves your Mac only to reach Google (the service you connect) and — only if you explicitly enable AI-assisted matching — Anthropic, as described in section 5. The developer receives nothing in either case.

2. How the app works

Contact SyncMate operates on your Mac, with no intermediary server. Data flows directly between:

  • Your Mac and Google’s servers (via the Google People API), and
  • Your Mac and Apple Contacts (via Apple’s Contacts framework, local or iCloud)
  • Your Mac and Anthropic’s API — only if you explicitly enable AI-assisted matching (section 5)

There is no intermediary server. The developer never sees your data.

3. Google account data we access

ScopePurpose
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts Read your Google contacts to detect changes since the last sync, and write (create / update / delete) Google contacts so edits made on your Mac are reflected in Google. Deletions are propagated only if you enable that option, and each is shown for confirmation first.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email Display which Google account is connected, inside the app’s Settings window.

No other scopes are requested. Google account data is used solely to provide contact synchronisation, and for no other purpose.

4. Where data is stored on your Mac

  • OAuth tokens — macOS Keychain (encrypted by the operating system). Never written in plain text.
  • Backup snapshots — a folder you choose (default ~/Documents/Contact SyncMate Backups), so any sync can be reviewed and undone.
  • Sync history and settings — ~/Library/Application Support/ and standard macOS preferences.

Backup snapshots and sync history are written to disk unencrypted — they contain contact data in plain form, protected only by macOS file permissions and FileVault if you have it enabled — and the app warns you if the backup folder you choose is synced to a cloud service, since that would copy the snapshots off your Mac.

All of this stays on your Mac and is under your control. Deleting the app and these folders removes everything.

5. Optional AI-assisted duplicate matching

Duplicate detection runs on-device by default. You may optionally supply your own Anthropic API key to improve matching on ambiguous pairs. Nothing is sent unless you also switch on the explicit consent toggle in Settings → AI Matching — entering an API key alone does not enable cloud processing. If — and only if — you give this consent:

  • Only the fields needed to compare two specific ambiguous records — names, organisation, job title, email addresses, and phone numbers — are sent to Anthropic’s API (api.anthropic.com).
  • No full contact list is ever transmitted.
  • The request goes directly from your Mac to Anthropic under your own account; the developer is not involved and never receives the data.
  • This feature is off by default and can be turned off at any time.

Your API key is stored in the macOS Keychain.

6. Third-party services

  • Google — subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — subject to Apple’s privacy terms for Contacts and iCloud.
  • Anthropic — only if you opt in to AI-assisted matching with your own key.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers, and we never sell data.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Contact SyncMate’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Your controls

  • Revoke access — at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or with the app’s Sign Out button (which also clears stored tokens from the Keychain).
  • Choose what syncs — enable or disable individual fields, restrict sync to selected groups or labels, and turn deletion propagation off.
  • Review before applying — preview every change before it is written.
  • Delete your data — remove the backup folder and the app’s Application Support folder.

9. Children’s privacy

Contact SyncMate is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
