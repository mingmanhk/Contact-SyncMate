Contact SyncMate

Two-way contact synchronisation between Google Contacts and Apple Contacts, for macOS.

What this app does

Contact SyncMate is a native macOS menu bar application that keeps your Google Contacts and your Apple Contacts (iCloud or “On My Mac”) in sync with each other.

Many people keep contacts in two places — a Google account used for mail and Android, and Apple Contacts used across Mac, iPhone and iPad. Over time the two lists drift apart: a phone number updated on one side never reaches the other, and the same person ends up duplicated. Contact SyncMate reconciles both lists and keeps them matching.

How data flows

Google Contacts ⇄ [ Your Mac — Contact SyncMate ] ⇄ Apple Contacts No server operated by the developer sits anywhere in this path.

What it does with your Google data

The app requests exactly two OAuth scopes, and nothing else:

ScopeWhy it is needed
auth/contacts Read — to list your Google contacts and detect what changed since the last sync.
Write — to create, update, and (only when you explicitly enable it and confirm) delete Google contacts, so that edits you made on your Mac reach Google. Two-way sync is impossible without write access; contacts.readonly would allow Google → Mac only.
auth/userinfo.email To display which Google account is currently connected in the app’s Settings window, so you can tell accounts apart.

Features

  • Two-way sync — or one-way (Google → Mac, Mac → Google) if you prefer a single source of truth.
  • Preview before applying — every add, update, delete and merge is listed for review; you can override any individual change.
  • Automatic backups — a snapshot is taken before and after each sync so any sync can be reviewed and rolled back.
  • Duplicate detection — on-device matching for nicknames, transposed names, phonetic surnames and phone-format differences. Optional cloud AI assistance is off by default and requires your own API key.
  • Field-level control — choose which fields participate (photos, birthdays, addresses, websites, job titles).
  • Scheduled sync — runs in the background at an interval you choose, with optional conditions (AC power, Wi-Fi only, idle only).
  • Change history — a per-contact log of exactly what the app changed and when.

Privacy

All synchronisation runs locally on your Mac. Your contacts are never transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any server operated by the developer or by any third party. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no sale of data. OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain, never in plain text. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page or with the app’s Sign Out button.
